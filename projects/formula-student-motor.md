# Formula Student traction motor

Detailed mechanical integration of an inherited IPMSM concept in PTC Creo, including rotor, stator, shaft, bearing arrangement, resolver and cooling passages. The electromagnetic concept was inherited from prior team research.

## Control development
Nonlinear FEA-derived motor modelling and offline norm-optimal harmonic iterative learning control for torque-ripple compensation. Model-based evaluation using Formula Student operating data; physical inverter validation remains future work.

CAD and manuscript assets are withheld pending publication clearance.
